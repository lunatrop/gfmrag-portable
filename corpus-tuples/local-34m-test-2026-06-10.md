## Caveats before concluding "8M is better"

1. **16 docs is far below the regime the 34M targets** — its advantage is
   multi-hop reasoning over large graphs; a tiny single-shard KG can't show it.
2. **Embedding-backend fidelity**: local stage-2 used the sentence-transformers
   fallback; production uses vLLM serving. Same weights, but pooling/precision
   differences could shift near-tied rankings (Q2's top-2 are 0.42 apart).
3. Scores are raw logits (negative = fine); only ordering matters.

## Verdict

8M is the right default for the small-corpus/local regime. Re-run this
comparison at rung 3+ (1k+ docs) and on multi-hop RelatedStatements queries
before deciding the production checkpoint — that's the 34M's home turf.

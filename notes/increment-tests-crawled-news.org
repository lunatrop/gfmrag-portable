How to test the incremental-indexing / compaction cycle when the corpora are
*arbitrary crawled prose* — supply-chain news, cybersecurity news, business
intelligence — rather than the structured register. The register tests leaned
on a free answer key (ABNs, RelatedStatements); crawled news has none, so the
test program is reconceived around different sources of truth and the failure
modes specific to this domain.

* The fundamental shift: no free answer key

The register gave ground truth for free (every passage came from a CSV row).
Arbitrary crawled news gives nothing. The verification question splits — and the
tests get "truth" from four sources instead:

1. *The source text itself* — for FAITHFULNESS (does an edge follow from its
   cited span). No external truth needed; the article is the authority.
2. *Controlled injection* — author synthetic articles with known facts,
   duplicates, collisions, contradictions. You own the truth, so stress tests
   become exact.
3. *KB anchors* — register/ABN (AU firms), Wikidata / SEC EDGAR (identity),
   domains/WHOIS, ticker symbols. Partial but hard truth for entity resolution.
4. *A small hand-labelled golden set* — the one place to spend on humans:
   multi-hop chains and ambiguous entities only.

* A. Correctness / functioning (mostly truth-free)

** A1 — Incremental ≡ batch
Build the graph incrementally vs as one batch over the same N crawled docs;
compare node/edge sets + a fixed query set's retrieval. Self-consistency, no
truth needed. The delta = drift.

** A2 — Faithfulness re-check (replaces the register fact-gate)
Every edge in the grown graph must trace to a source span that ENTAILS it
(provenance + NLI / LLM-judge). Metric: % of edges that re-pass span-entailment.
Core correctness metric for prose; needs no external truth. Increments must
never admit ungrounded edges.

** A3 — Entity-consistency invariant
No two nodes share a HARD identifier (canonical domain, ticker, ABN, EDGAR CIK)
yet remain unmerged. Uses strong signals as partial truth. Metric:
duplicate-node rate by identifier.

** A4 — Idempotency & order-independence
Re-adding an article (or its syndicated copy) creates nothing new; crawl order
doesn't change the graph. Far more important here than for the register — see C3.

** A5 — Snapshot isolation + retraction
Serving stays on a consistent snapshot during a merge; retracting a source
removes its uncorroborated claims and compaction reclaims the dead edges.

* B. Advantage (the product's reason to exist)

** B1 — Cross-corpus multi-hop (the value claim)
Killer queries span genres: "which insured prospects are <=2 hops from this
breached vendor?" — cyber breach article -> supply-chain vendor-relationship
article -> business-intel customer record. Answerable ONLY on the unified
incrementally-grown graph, not isolated corpora. Truth: curated golden set of
hand-verified multi-hop chains (e.g. the VIQ->e24 subcontracting-breach chain).
This is where human labelling spend goes — it is the core value.

** B2 — Cost curve O(new) vs O(total)
Per-increment wall-clock + GPU, incremental-add vs method-2-rebuild, plotted
against accumulated graph size. Incremental stays ~flat; method-2 rises. No
truth needed.

** B3 — Drift + compaction recovery
Drift (A1 delta + A3 duplicate rate) accumulates over many increments; compaction
restores batch-equivalence. Calibrates compaction cadence.

** B4 — Corroboration counts INDEPENDENT sources
Inject a true event corroborated across genuinely independent outlets vs a single
rumor; verify the corroboration score rises only with independence (requires C3).

* C. Domain stress tests (the crawled-news realities)

** C1 — Entity ambiguity & collision (hardest, most important)
No universal ID in news. Inject: two different companies sharing a name ("Acme"
US vs AU); aliases of one company (legal name / ticker / common name /
subsidiary "Microsoft Australia Pty Ltd"). Verify collisions SPLIT and aliases
MERGE, anchoring to register/Wikidata/domain. False-merge here = a breach
attributed to the wrong company = defamation. Truth: curated alias/collision set.

** C2 — Temporal correctness
Events are dated and recur. Two breaches of one vendor in different years; queries
like "breached in the last 12 months". Verify edges carry time and time-filtered
multi-hop doesn't conflate distinct events. Truth: dated golden events.

** C3 — Syndication / near-duplicate resistance
A wire story republished across 50 outlets must count as ONE source for
corroboration, not 50, and must not 50x the graph. Verify near-dup detection.
Truth: known syndicated sets.

** C4 — Source-reliability weighting
Same claim from an SEC filing vs an anonymous blog — provenance must carry
reliability and the release gate must weight accordingly.

** C5 — Adversarial defamation-precision (legally critical, zero tolerance)
Red-team set of plausible-but-unsupported ADVERSE claims ("Company X was
breached") that are ungrounded, single-source, or contradicted. Verify NONE pass
the outward gate (entity exact-match + span-grounded + corroborated). Metric:
false-assertion rate on adverse claims must be ~0%. The cost of failure is a
lawsuit, not a quality dip.

** C6 — Genre robustness
pipeline.org found opinion/LinkedIn text -> wrong-binding hallucination (67%
arity failure). Commentary crawled alongside news will misbehave. Verify the
faithfulness gate (A2) catches wrong-binding on opinion content. Keep opinion
docs permanently in the golden set.

** C7 — Cross-article coreference
"the vendor", "it", partial names resolved to canonical nodes across documents —
the bridge that makes multi-hop work when articles don't repeat full names.

** C8 — Realistic-velocity simulation
Replay a bursty, duplicated, mixed-quality crawl stream over time; measure
entity-resolution drift and the compaction cadence needed to hold quality. The
production dress-rehearsal.

* The reprioritisation vs the register

For the register, entity resolution was easy (ABN anchor) and focus was
extraction recall. For crawled supply-chain / cyber / BI news the priority
inverts:

1. *C1 entity resolution* — dominant risk; no universal ID, false-merge is a
   defamation vector.
2. *C5 defamation-precision* — zero-tolerance outward gate on adverse claims.
3. *C3 syndication dedup* — news is massively duplicated; silently corrupts
   corroboration.
4. *B1 cross-corpus multi-hop* — the value actually being sold.

Everything else (cost curve, drift, equivalence) is the same machinery as the
register tests; what changes is the TRUTH strategy (faithfulness + injection +
KB anchors + small golden set) and these four domain risks.
